How a fact gets here

  1. Collect. Company filings, state policies, property and salary reports, press releases.
  2. Extract. AI pulls out each number, what it counts, its date and its publisher.
  3. Check. A person matches it against the original page before it goes live.
  4. Publish with sources. Every number keeps all its sources, a status, and our reading.

Our one rule

Nobody can pay to change our data or rankings.

Providers, brokers and states can pay to reach companies, always labelled as paid. They can never pay to look better. Our own data team works apart from sales, and our method is public.

Questions

What is a GCC?

A Global Capability Centre is an office a foreign company runs in India with its own staff, for technology, engineering, finance or operations work. Starbucks in Chennai and DoorDash in Hyderabad are recent examples.

How do you decide which number is right?

We don't hide disagreement. Every source's number is shown with what it counts, its date and its publisher. Where they conflict, we explain why and give a range. A person checks each fact against the original page before it goes live.

Can a provider or state pay to rank higher?

No. They can pay to reach companies, clearly labelled as paid. They can never pay to change data or rankings.

Where does the AI come in?

AI helps collect sources and extract numbers, and Ask GroundTruth answers questions only from checked facts, citing each one. It never publishes a fact on its own.

Is the setup roadmap legal advice?

No. It's a guide built from published rules and adviser checklists, with sources. Confirm your dates with a company secretary or lawyer.
